TREASURE HUNT - game prototype
==============================

HOW TO OPEN
Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser.
No installation and no internet connection are needed.
Use an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

THE CHAPTERS
index.html    Chapter One, "Home Alone": the brother and sister are locked in the house
              and must find the spare key. Getting out leads on to Chapter Two.
city.html     Chapter Two, "Across the City": a long journey across town to the Old Bridge:
              a torn map, road crossings, a shutter, a runaway puppy, a canal ferry,
              the metro, a bus and a lift bridge. About 15 to 20 minutes.
              It ends at Forest Park, which leads on to the forest.
forest.html   The Forest Trail (the first art scene; the next chapter will be built here).

CONTROLS (two players on one keyboard)
Sister:   A / D walk     W use, hold buttons, go upstairs     S go downstairs, crawl, look in
Brother:  Left / Right walk     Up use, hold buttons, go upstairs     Down go downstairs, crawl in
Pressing W or Up with nothing nearby makes that character cheer.
A hint over each character's head shows what they can do where they stand.
On a phone or tablet, on-screen buttons appear instead.

HOW THE PUZZLES WORK (spoilers)
The sister is tall: she reaches things that are high up.
The brother is small: he fits through small doors and gaps.
Chapter One: he finds the box code in the dark cupboard, she gets Dad's box from the bookcase.
Chapter Two: the wind tears the map, and the pieces land where only one of them can reach; wait
for the green man; hold the shutter button for each other; the brother crawls under the courtyard
gate; corner the puppy from both sides; send the ferry across, back, and across again; read the
traffic lights on Grand Avenue; on the metro, Blue line towards Harbour, change at Central, Green
line to Old Town; trace the right bus on the route map; the sister switches on the bridge power and
the brother lowers the bridge from the little hut.
S or Down with nothing nearby shows the treasure map (or the metro map, underground).

WHAT IS IN THE FOLDER
index.html, city.html, forest.html   the chapters; open index.html to start
worlds/        one folder per world (house, city, forest): its art, its settings and its code.
               worlds/README.md explains how to change or replace any of it.
shared/        what every world uses: the two characters and the shared play code
tools/         developer tools used to create the artwork and test the game; not needed to play
