# Level 3, the canal: first-time player review (2026-11-25)

I went through the level as somebody who has just finished Level 2 (the city, which ends at the Old Bridge) and has
never seen the canal: every thing that is drawn, moves, sounds or reacts. For each one: could it distract, look like
something to use when it is not, or need explaining? And what I recommend: **keep**, **tone down**, **move** or
**remove**.

**How it was done.** From the code, from pictures of every section at computer, tablet and phone size, and from the
two scripted tests. Not with real players: where I write "could", that is a guess to check when the client plays.

**What was applied.** Only fixes that were clearly needed; those rows say **Applied**. Everything else is left for you
to decide. Each row has a number, so you can answer with a list.

## The six I would decide first

| # | What | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Level 2 already has small boats, a ferry and the same Old Bridge** | Light-only surfaces are not new to somebody coming from the city, and the chapter crosses the same bridge twice. |
| 12 | **In section 5 he can stand on the plate himself** | Then the box is not needed. Both ways need both siblings, but the "carry" part of the twist can be skipped. |
| 2 | **No toss in this level** | Players learned it one level earlier. Here grab on her shoulders just sets him down. |
| 9 | **Getting onto the low end of the see-saw** | Walking onto it only works at its very tip; jumping onto it works anywhere. Same see-saw as Level 2. |
| 11 | **The calm moment has no dog and no ball** | The brief made them optional. I left them out; fetch would be a new interaction to learn. |
| 4 | **Whoever runs ahead stops at the edge of the picture** | Nothing shows why. Known from Level 1's review; here it matters more, because the two are often on different banks. |

## The whole level

| # | What it is and where | Could it distract, look usable, need explaining? | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What Level 2 already shows.** The city has a canal station with small blue boats that sink under her and a ferry on a lever, a see-saw, and ends by lowering the Old Bridge with two levers. | The brief treats light-only surfaces as Level 3's new idea and the Old Bridge as the chapter's finale. For a player they are a second meeting. It still works as a level (the plank is new, and so are the combinations), but the "one new idea" is thinner than planned. | **Decide.** Either accept it, or later end Level 2 before its canal station and at a different place than the bridge. Not applied: Level 2 was not to be changed. |
| 2 | **No toss, no pulley, no heavy crate.** The brief lists the tools for Level 3; these three from Level 2 are not on it. Grab on her shoulders sets him down. | A player who liked the toss will try it and find it gone. With the toss the high shelf in section 6 could be reached without the see-saw. | **Keep** off. If the toss should stay, section 6 needs a higher shelf. |
| 3 | **The lift** works everywhere and is needed nowhere. Its two-hands sign is not shown in this level. | Lifting him does not help at any puzzle (checked by the solo test). Without the sign nobody is led to try. | **Keep.** |
| 4 | **The edge of the picture.** In Levels 1 and 2 the picture follows the middle of the two and pushes the one behind along. | At tablet width, when he walked ahead over the boats, the picture pushed her off the quay into the river, again and again (found by the play-through at 1024x768). | **Applied:** in this level the picture never pushes the one behind; whoever runs ahead stops at its edge. Still open, as in Level 1: a small picture that shows why. |
| 5 | **Falling in.** A splash, a duck's quack, the other sibling starts; 0.7 seconds later the one who fell is back: beside the partner if both are on the same bank, otherwise on the bank they came from. | The brief says "next to their partner". Across the water that would be a way round every puzzle, so there they return to their own bank. | **Keep.** |
| 6 | **Words on the houses**: Books, Café, Bakery, News, Rose Court, and "Bridge Control" on the hut. The pictures are the city's. | Nobody has to read them. | **Keep** for now, as in Levels 1 and 2. |
| 7 | **The canal in front of the quay**, with slow glints. | It is the foreground, like the road in the city: nobody can step into it. A child might still try to walk "down" into it. | **Keep.** Watch. |

## Section by section

| # | What it is and where | Could it distract, look usable, need explaining? | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Section 1, the first screen**: the quay, two blue plates, one gate, the thought bubble with a gate. No lamp, no bench, nobody else. | Plain. It is Level 1's first puzzle again, so it is solved from memory: 11 seconds for the script, against 18 for the last puzzle of Level 2. | **Keep.** |
| 9 | **The see-saw** (sections 3 and 6). Its blue end rests low; her jump onto the green end sends him flying, and he lands where he should without steering. | Walking onto the blue end only works at its last few steps; from the middle he walks underneath it. Jumping onto it works anywhere. It is the kit's see-saw, as in Level 2. | **Applied:** the hint's ghost and sparkle now stand at the tip, where stepping on works. **Tone down** later: a see-saw whose low end can be walked onto from anywhere (a change to the shared kit, so also to Level 2). |
| 10 | **Section 2, the thin plank.** Under her it creaks, bends deep and walks her back; she gets a strained face. A jump onto it springs her back to her bank. | Clear, and funny. While the solo test was being written it found that she could cross by jumping the instant her feet touched a plank or a boat. | **Applied:** nobody too heavy can jump off a light-only surface, and the plank always sends her back to the bank she last stood on. |
| 11 | **Section 4, the calm moment**: three ducks, a brown rowing boat tied to the quay, a bench. Nothing can be used. | The bench could look like something to jump onto (it is only a picture). | 15 | **Section 6, the box as a step.** It need not touch the ledge: anywhere within a short jump works. | Forgiving on purpose. On a slow device the script puts it 60 short of the ledge and still gets up. | **Keep.** |
