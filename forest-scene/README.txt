FOREST TRAIL - scene prototype
==============================

HOW TO OPEN
Unzip the folder, then double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser.
No installation and no internet connection are needed.
Use an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

CONTROLS
Hooded traveller:  A / D to walk,  W to cheer
Lantern bearer:    Left / Right arrow keys to walk,  Up arrow to cheer
On a phone or tablet, on-screen buttons appear instead.

WHAT TO TRY
- Walk the two characters apart and their faces turn worried.
- Stand them next to each other and they smile and giggle.
- Leave them alone for about 10 seconds and they get sleepy and yawn.

WHAT IS IN THE FOLDER
index.html   the scene; open this one
assets/      all the artwork as separate image files (trees, plants, rocks, waterfall, sky...).
             assets/README.md explains how to replace any of them.
js/          the code that runs the scene
tools/       developer tools used to create the artwork; not needed to view the scene
