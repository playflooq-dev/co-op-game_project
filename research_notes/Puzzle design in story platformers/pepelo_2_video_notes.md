# Pepelo 2: Around the World — notes from watching a full walkthrough

Source: "PEPELO 2 || AROUND THE WORLD - FULL WALKTHROUGH SPEEDRUN (Android,IOS)", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ghz5wP6Ae50 (69 min).
Method: still frames every 25 s across the whole video, plus every 4 s around eight unclear puzzles (2026-11-19).
No store page, review or article describes the puzzles, so everything below is read from the frames. The mechanics
are inferred from still pictures, not from playing.

Game: Tafusoft, released Jan 2026, iOS/Android, 3D with a mostly side-on camera, 2 players (online or one person
switching). Both characters (a boy and a girl) look like they have the SAME abilities: walk, jump, use, plus an attack
button. The co-op comes from where each one stands and what they push or ride, not from different powers.
Each level is a real place. A green pickup truck drives the pair between levels. Coins and costumes are the rewards.

Takeaways for our game:
- Same abilities for both means the co-op is mostly positional: plates, counterweights, see-saws, one rides while the
  other works something. Our siblings' different bodies (tall and small) can make these stronger.
- Each level is one well-known place with one or two signature gadgets. Rides (truck, cable car, boat, jet ski) are the
  calm moments between puzzles.
- It relies on jumping, carrying, and enemies to fight. Our rules leave these out (decisions 17, 20).
